DDdesk.ai
Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version: 1.0
Status: Product Planning
Target Market: India — Initial focus on Rajasthan
Target Launch: Diwali 2026
Product Type: Multi-tenant SaaS Platform
Primary Users: Study Library Owners, Staff, Students
1. Product Overview
DDdesk.ai is a simple digital management platform designed for study libraries, reading rooms, self-study centres, and competitive-exam libraries.
DDdesk.ai allows a library owner to manage the complete library from one system: library management, student management, seats, attendance, memberships, fees and payments, pending/credit fees, renewals, library website, student mobile application, stories, notices, banners, offers, complaints, reports, notifications, business intelligence, and AI assistance.
The product should remain simple for the library owner even though the underlying system is technically advanced.
2. Product Vision
Make every study library digitally manageable without requiring technical knowledge.
DDdesk should help the owner quickly understand: how many seats are occupied, how much money has been collected, who has pending fees, which memberships are expiring, and what needs attention today.
3. Product Mission
Help library owners save time, reduce manual registers, reduce fee-collection problems, reduce empty seats, improve student communication, increase renewals, get new students, and build a professional online presence.
4. Target Customers
Primary customers are small and medium-sized private study libraries, reading rooms, and self-study centres, from roughly 30 seats to 200+ seats.
Initial geography: Rajasthan, with potential early focus on Udaipur, Jaipur, Kota, Ajmer, Bhilwara, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Sikar, and Alwar. After validation, expand to other Indian states.
5. Target Users
Super Admin — controls the DDdesk platform.
Library Owner — owns and manages a library.
Library Staff — helps with daily operations.
Student — uses the library and student application.
6. Product Architecture
DDdesk is a multi-tenant SaaS application. One DDdesk platform can contain many libraries.
Each library gets isolated data and a dynamic website/subdomain such as gyan.dddesk.ai, success.dddesk.ai, or xyz.dddesk.ai.
Library A must never access Library B's students, payments, attendance, fees, reports, staff, or website settings.
7. Library Website
Every approved library receives a dynamic website generated from its DDdesk data.
Website sections: Home, About, Facilities, Gallery, Membership Plans, Available Seats, Offers, Stories, Notices, Contact, Location, and Student Registration.
Website features: logo, library name, cover/banner, photos, facilities, timings, pricing, available seats, online enquiry, online admission, online payment, WhatsApp contact, and Google Maps location.
Future: custom domain support such as www.gyanstudylibrary.com.
8. Super Admin Requirements
Dashboard: total libraries, active libraries, pending approvals, expired subscriptions, revenue, new registrations, active students, and system activity.
Library management: view, search, filter, approve, reject, suspend, activate, and view library details.
Subscription management: plans, pricing, trial period, subscription status, payment history, renewal, and expiry.
System management: notifications, support, global settings, activity logs, and website/domain management.
9. Library Registration
Owner registration fields: library name, owner name, mobile, email, address, city, state, pincode, and number of seats. Optional: logo, cover image, photos, and facilities.
Flow: Owner Registration → Verification → Pending Approval → Super Admin Review → Approved → Library Account Created → Subdomain Created.
10. Library Profile
Owner can manage library name, logo, cover image, about, address, Google Maps, phone, email, opening/closing time, facilities, rules, gallery, and social media.
11. Seat Management
Create floors, rooms, halls, and seats. Seat information includes seat number, floor, room, type, price, and status.
Seat types: Normal, AC, Premium, Window, and Custom.
Seat status: Available, Occupied, Reserved, Blocked, and Maintenance.
Advanced features: live occupancy, seat transfer, seat history, no-show detection, automatic release, empty-seat alerts, occupancy forecast, and smart seat recommendation.
12. Student Management
Owner can add, edit, view, search, block, activate, assign seats, change seats, and assign memberships.
Student information: name, photo, mobile, email, address, emergency contact, joining date, membership, seat, shift, and status.
13. Membership Management
Create daily, weekly, monthly, 3-month, 6-month, yearly, and custom plans.
Plans can include price, duration, seat type, shift, facilities, and discount.
14. Fee Management
DDdesk must support full payment, partial payment, pending payment, credit payment, offline payment, and online payment.
15. Credit / Pending Fee Requirement
A student may join without paying immediately. Example: student joins on 1 October, monthly fee is ₹600, and asks to pay next month.
System records: Total Fee ₹600, Paid ₹0, Pending ₹600, Status Credit, Due Date 1 November.
The student may continue using the library according to the owner's credit policy.
16. Partial Payment
Example: fee ₹600, student pays ₹300. System shows Total ₹600, Paid ₹300, Pending ₹300, Status Partial. When the remaining ₹300 is paid, status becomes Paid.
17. Multiple Pending Months
Example: October ₹600 pending and November ₹600 new fee. Total Due becomes ₹1,200. When payment is received, the due balance is updated and payment history is retained.
18. Credit Policy
Owner can configure: allow credit, maximum credit amount, maximum credit days, grace period, reminder frequency, and whether membership can be blocked after the due date.
The system should not automatically block every student; the owner controls the final action.
19. Payment Management
Payment methods: cash, UPI, online payment, bank transfer, and other.
Online payment should support payment creation, success, failure, webhook verification, refund, and payment history. Generate a digital receipt after successful payment.
20. Attendance Management
Attendance methods: QR, manual, student app, and future biometric integration.
Records: check-in, check-out, late, early exit, absent, and leave.
21. Student App
Keep the application simple. Main screens: Dashboard, My Seat, Attendance, Fees, Payments, Renew, Stories, Notices, Offers, Complaints, and Profile.
22. Student Dashboard
Show student name, library, seat, membership status, validity, pending fee, attendance, stories, latest notice, and quick actions.
23. Library Stories
Owner can upload image, short video, text, offer, or announcement. Stories appear in the student app and may automatically expire after 24 hours.
24. Notices
Owner can create general notices, holidays, timing changes, fee reminders, exam notices, maintenance notices, emergency notices, and important announcements.
Notices appear in the student app and library website.
25. Banners
Owner can create promotional banners with image, title, description, start date, end date, button, link, and status.
Banners can appear on the library website and student app.
26. Offers & Coupons
Create new-admission, festival, referral, first-month, limited-seat offers, and coupon codes.
27. Enquiry / Admission
Student visits the library website and clicks Join Now. Flow: Registration → Enquiry → Owner Notification → Approval → Membership → Seat → Payment/Credit → Student Account.
28. Notifications
Events include new admission, payment received, payment pending, membership expiry, renewal, new notice, new story, new offer, and complaint updates.
Possible channels: push notification, WhatsApp, SMS, and email.
29. Complaint & Maintenance
Students can report AC, fan, light, Wi-Fi, chair, table, charging point, washroom, or other issues.
Workflow: Open → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved.
30. Owner Dashboard
Keep the dashboard simple. Show total seats, occupied seats, available seats, today's collection, pending fees, new enquiries, expiring memberships, and open complaints.
Quick actions: Add Student, Add Fee, Add Notice, Upload Story, Upload Banner, View Pending Fees, and View Empty Seats.
31. Reports
Student reports: active students, new students, expired students, renewals.
Seat reports: total seats, occupied, available, occupancy.
Attendance reports: daily, monthly, student-wise, shift-wise.
Finance reports: daily collection, monthly collection, pending fees, credit students, partial payments, payment history, refunds.
Website reports: enquiries, registrations, admission requests.
32. Business Intelligence
DDdesk can provide occupancy forecasts, empty-seat alerts, renewal alerts, pending-fee alerts, and smart-offer suggestions.
33. DDdesk AI Assistant
Owner can ask: How is my library doing? How much did I collect this month? Who has pending fees? Which memberships expire this week? How many seats are empty? Why did revenue decrease?
AI answers using the library's own authorized data.
34. Staff Management
Owner can create Manager, Receptionist, and Staff users. Permissions can control access to students, seats, attendance, fees, complaints, and reports. Important staff activity should be logged.
35. Multi-Branch Management
For larger customers, support multiple branches under one owner account, with branch-level students, seats, staff, fees, and performance.
36. Student Feedback
Students can submit ratings, feedback, suggestions, and complaints. Owners can review feedback.
37. Security Requirements
Secure authentication, OTP/login, password hashing, role-based permissions, tenant isolation, API authorization, input validation, rate limiting, secure file uploads, secure payment webhooks, audit logs, database backups, HTTPS, and secure secrets management.
38. Multi-Tenant Security
Every business record must belong to a library. The API must verify that the logged-in user's library ID matches the requested data's library ID. If not, access must be rejected.
39. Subscription System
DDdesk will have subscription plans for library owners. Flow: Free Trial → Choose Plan → Payment → Subscription Active → Renewal. Possible plans: Starter, Professional, Business. Final pricing should be validated with pilot libraries.
40. Technical Requirements
Recommended initial technology: Next.js/React/TypeScript for web; Node.js/Express/TypeScript for backend; MongoDB for database; React Native for mobile; Cloudinary/object storage for media; Razorpay for payments; Firebase Cloud Messaging plus WhatsApp/SMS providers for notifications.
41. Environments
Maintain Development, Staging, and Production environments. Production must not be used for development testing.
42. Domain Structure
Main: dddesk.ai. Admin: admin.dddesk.ai. API: api.dddesk.ai. Library websites: gyan.dddesk.ai, success.dddesk.ai, xyz.dddesk.ai. Wildcard DNS should support *.dddesk.ai.
43. Development Workflow
Requirement → UI/UX → Database → API → Frontend → Integration → Testing → Code Review → Staging → Production.
Git branches: main, develop, feature/*, hotfix/*.
44. Testing Requirements
Test unit, API, integration, UI, permission, and security scenarios.
Payment tests: full payment, partial payment, credit, pending, refund, failed payment, duplicate payment, and webhook failure.
Tenant tests: Library A cannot access Library B; students cannot access other students; staff cannot access unauthorized modules.
45. Production Monitoring
Monitor API health, website uptime, database, server resources, storage, application errors, payment failures, and notification failures. Use centralized application/error logging.
46. Backup
Production database must have automatic backups, retention rules, a restore process, and regular restore testing.
47. Pilot Launch
Start with approximately 5–10 real libraries. Test students, seats, attendance, fees, credit payments, renewals, website, stories, notices, and student app. Collect real feedback before scaling.
48. Production Launch
Pilot → Fix bugs → Improve UX → Security review → Production deployment → Public registration → Subscription activation → Customer support.
49. MVP Scope
Must-have: Super Admin, library registration/approval, Library Admin, profile, dynamic subdomain, library website, student management, seat management, membership plans, attendance, fees, credit/pending fees, partial payments, renewals, student app, stories, notices, banners, enquiries, notifications, basic reports, staff, and security.
50. Post-MVP Features
Occupancy forecasting, smart seat recovery, no-show auto-release, renewal risk, smart offers, business health, AI assistant, multi-branch, custom domains, advanced analytics, exam/current-affairs features, and AI study assistant.
51. Success Metrics
Business: registered libraries, active libraries, paid libraries, subscription renewal rate.
Library usage: active students, seat occupancy, fee collection, renewal rate, pending fees.
Product: daily active owners, daily active students, website enquiries, app usage, notification delivery.
52. Final Product Principle
Simple interface, powerful system.
The owner should see: Dashboard, Students, Seats, Attendance, Fees, Content, Reports, Settings.
Behind the simple interface, DDdesk handles multi-tenancy, payments, credit fees, partial payments, notifications, subdomains, website generation, analytics, AI, security, and audit logs.
53. Complete DDdesk Flow
Library Owner → Register → Super Admin Approval → Library Account → xyz.dddesk.ai → Library Setup → Seats + Plans → Student Registration → Membership → Seat Assignment → Attendance → Fee → Paid / Partial / Credit → Reminder → Renewal → Reports → Business Insights.
54. Product Definition
DDdesk.ai is a simple multi-tenant SaaS platform that gives study-library owners their own digital website, student app, and complete library-management system.
For owners: Manage the library.
For students: Manage membership and stay connected with the library.
For DDdesk: Help libraries manage better, collect fees on time, reduce empty seats, and grow.
